ClimateConnect: how the prototype changed

Two versions of the same app. Each opens as its own site, so you can try them side by side.

VERSION 1First version

Weather on the home screen

Starting point, with an everyday weather entry point.

  • Apple-style weather card
  • Walking character who dresses for the weather
  • Bird companion and dialogue
  • Source-linked facts, feedback form, challenges

VERSION 2Current

Climate information verification app

Changed after prototype review.

  • Home leads with a claim to check: source, date, publisher
  • Verify page: check any claim you heard in four steps
  • Simple or detailed explanations, less jargon
  • Feedback on one claim, sent for review, with a receipt
  • Community forum
  • Rewards kept out of trust ratings
  • Weather moved to local context
